Rooftop Rumble

Six fighters, five stages, best of three. Click the stage once so it can hear your keyboard.

Move list

  • Combos: press a fighter's combo buttons in order, quickly. When a hit connects, the next hit cancels straight in, and the last press fires the finisher.
  • Tether's rope spear: after it hits, hold toward to reel them in, away to throw them over you, up to lift them, or down to slam them.
  • Block by holding away from your opponent. Hold down as well to stop low attacks. Down + kick sweeps, down + heavy is an uppercut.
  • Kestrel and Kage can double jump. Kage can also throw shuriken in the air. Mammoth shrugs off hits during his heavy punch and charge.
  • Esc pauses and shows both move lists (Backspace then quits to the menu). M mutes everything, N toggles just the music.
